Two standalone teaching notebooks written to accompany blog posts in 2018.

**Collaborative filtering for implicit feedback.** Most recommendation methods assume ratings — a user says a film
was worth four stars. Implicit feedback has no such signal: you only observe that someone played a track or clicked
a link, and crucially you never observe a genuine negative, since not clicking something might just mean never
seeing it. This notebook demonstrates the standard treatment, which weights observed interactions by a confidence
that grows with how often they occurred.

**Parallelisation for beginners.** A practical introduction to splitting independent computations across processes,
aimed at people whose analysis has outgrown a single core.
